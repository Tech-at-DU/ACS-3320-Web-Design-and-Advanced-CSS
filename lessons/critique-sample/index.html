About the Garden

Our garden is run entirely by volunteers. Members rent a raised bed for the season and share common tools, water, and compost. We hold workdays on the first Saturday of every month, and everyone is welcome — even if you don't have a plot. Bring gloves, bring a friend, and bring your curiosity. Our experienced gardeners love to share tips on soil, seeds, and keeping the squirrels away from your tomatoes. In the past year we donated more than 900 pounds of fresh produce to the Fernwood Food Pantry, and we hope to beat that number this season with your help.
