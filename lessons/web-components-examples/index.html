Experiments

  1. In DevTools, change --hue on :root from 250 to 30. The cards change too: tokens cross the shadow boundary.
  2. In fw-card.js, move the <h3> out of the slot and type it directly into the template. Is it still the accent color? Why not?
  3. Delete the <script> tag and reload. The cards disappear. Find the rule in framework.css that hides them and comment it out. What does an undefined custom element look like?
